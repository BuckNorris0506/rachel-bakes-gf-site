What Weak Sites Cost You

Good crews lose work every week because the website and intake path create hesitation. Visitors bounce or choose the competitor who makes trust and next steps obvious. These are the patterns we fix.

Slow mobile load

Heavy sliders and uncompressed photos mean a homeowner on their phone waits 7–10 seconds. By then they’ve tapped back and picked a competitor.

Weak call-to-action placement

Phone number buried in the footer. Buttons say “Learn More” instead of “Call Now” or “Request an Estimate.” Homeowners shouldn’t have to hunt.

Poor service page structure

One generic “Services” page instead of clear pages per job type. Google and homeowners both get confused. Strong service pages line up with how people search.

Visitors leave without contacting

No proof, no simple way to ask “Can you come look at this?” The website doesn’t close the gap between interest and calling.

What We Fix in a Rebuild

  • → Proof and trust structure — surface reviews, projects, process, and credibility where decisions happen.
  • → Clear inquiry path — call/text/request flows that remove friction and set response expectations.
  • → Service clarity — pages organized so buyers instantly see “you do my job.”
  • → Fast, mobile-first execution — lightweight and built for phones (no 10-second waits).
