Why DMs break down

Most pickup bakeries are not short on customers — they are short on a single place where orders, timing, and bake-day work actually line up.

Orders scattered

Instagram, Facebook, texts, email — hard to search and easy to miss.

Same answers on repeat

Pickup windows, what is available, and policies get retyped every week.

Bake day guesswork

You know orders exist — but totals by item are still mental math and scrolling.

Closed without a line

When ordering is off, interested buyers vanish unless you capture them somewhere real.

What you get

Everything below is aimed at preorder pickup — not corporate catering workflows.

  • Preorder storefront Menu-led, built for how small bakeries actually sell by the week.
  • Custom order requests Structured asks instead of long DM threads.
  • Open / close + capacity Match real bake load and pickup days.
  • Waitlist Capture interest when the kitchen is full or closed.
  • Pickup + policies on-site Fewer one-off explanations.
  • Owner command center Orders, bake totals, status, customer emails, profit snapshot (where configured).

Owner side

The command center is the point

Taking the order is not the same as knowing what to bake, what is confirmed, what is ready, and what customers still need to hear. BakeSites is built around that second job.

  • Orders view — day and status filters.
  • Bake totals by item — production for pickup date.
  • Status workflow — move orders through the kitchen.
  • Ready emails — pickup messaging.
  • Ordering controls — on/off, caps, messaging.
  • Profit snapshot — where numbers are configured.

Who this is for

Home bakers, cottage producers, cookie makers, custom cake bakers, and any pickup-first bakery that has outgrown inbox roulette. If you sell locally by preorder, this is the lane BakeSites is built for.
