The process

How BuckSites Works

Sometimes the fix is a focused page and an inquiry-path improvement. Other times the leak is the site structure itself, and the right move is a rebuild. Either way, we solve the real problem first — trust, clarity, and lead follow-up.

1

Diagnose the leak

I review your website and intake flow like a real prospect: trust proof, clarity, friction before inquiry, and what happens after someone calls or fills out a form.

You get a plain-language diagnosis — not jargon.

2

Prioritize the fix

We pick the highest-leverage change first — the one most likely to stop drop-offs and recover leads from the traffic and attention you already have.

We decide what to keep, what to change, and what to replace.

3

Install the solution

Then I implement the fix: proof structure, clearer pages, a cleaner request/quote flow, missed-call follow-up, lead routing, or a rebuild when that’s the right move.

The goal is fewer hesitations and fewer lost inquiries.

Step 4

Review and tighten

After the implementation is live, we sanity-check the path end-to-end and remove remaining friction so the fix holds up in the real world.
