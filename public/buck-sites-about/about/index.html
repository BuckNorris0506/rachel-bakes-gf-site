About

Why I Focus on Contractor Websites

Before building websites, I worked in foundation repair sales. Most days were spent crawling around basements and crawlspaces — then sitting at the kitchen table explaining options and pricing.

Over and over, homeowners told me the same thing when I asked how they found us: “We just looked up companies online.” They’d mention specific phrases they typed, how quickly a site loaded, or how clear it was to request an inspection.

I saw great crews lose out on easy jobs because their competitor’s website simply made it easier to call. BuckSites exists to close that gap — especially for owner-led and local teams who take pride in their work but don’t want to become marketers or software experts.
