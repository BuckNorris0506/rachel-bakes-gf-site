The problem

Why Good Jobs Slip Away

Homeowners with real projects choose someone else when your site and follow-up don’t keep up. Common leaks we fix in the pilot:

  • Weak trust — Site doesn’t feel established or credible enough for a big-ticket estimate.
  • Weak gallery proof — Project photos are buried, generic, or not organized by service so homeowners can’t see “my kind of job.”
  • Weak estimate path — No clear way to request an estimate; “Contact” is vague and doesn’t set expectations.
  • Missed calls — You’re on the job; calls go to voicemail and never get a fast callback or text.
  • Slow or messy follow-up — Leads sit in email or scribbled notes; no simple flow to respond and schedule.
