How preorder works

  1. 1. Check the menu and decide what you’d like.
  2. 2. Set quantities for pretzel bites, cinnamon rolls, cream pies, and/or dinner rolls; pick your date and window; choose payment preference — then send the request.
  3. 3. We’ll confirm availability and your order; payment details are finalized after approval.

Pickup basics

  • Where: Liberty, Missouri — exact pickup details are sent when your order is confirmed.
  • When: Windows depend on the bake schedule; weekends and holidays can shift availability.
  • Confirmation: Your request isn’t final until we reply — we’ll confirm details, pickup, and payment.
