Order / Preorder

Order ahead, pick up in Liberty.

Most items are made to order. Send us what you’d like and when you can pick up; we’ll confirm availability and get you on the schedule.

How preorder works

  1. 1. Check the menu and decide what you’d like.
  2. 2. Send your request with what you want, your preferred pickup date, and any notes.
  3. 3. We’ll confirm what’s available and coordinate pickup with you.

Pickup basics

  • Pickup is in Liberty, Missouri. We’ll share the exact details when your order is confirmed.
  • Pickup windows follow our bake schedule—weekends, holidays, and events can shift timing.
  • What’s available can change week to week; we’ll let you know when we confirm.
