About

Gluten-free bakery, pickup in Liberty.

Rachel Bakes GF is a home bakery in Liberty, Missouri. We do preorders and pickup—you tell us what you’d like and when you can pick up, and we’ll confirm. The menu is built around the things people order most: pretzel bites, cinnamon rolls, seasonal specials, and the occasional custom cake when it’s a good fit.

Everything is gluten-free. We keep the process simple so you can see what’s available, how pickup works, and what to expect—no complicated checkout, just clear requests and a reliable pickup.
