About

Gluten-free bakery, pickup in Liberty.

Rachel Bakes GF is a home bakery in Liberty, Missouri. We do preorders and pickup—you tell us what you’d like and when you can pick up, and we’ll confirm. The menu stays intentionally small: pretzel bites, cinnamon rolls, oatmeal cream pies, dinner rolls, and carrot cake by custom inquiry when it’s a good fit.

Everything is gluten-free. We keep the process simple so you can see what’s available, how pickup works, and what to expect—no complicated checkout, just clear requests and a reliable pickup.
