Custom Orders

Carrot cake & custom inquiries when the fit is right.

Carrot cake is our custom offering on the menu—everything else goes through preorder. We take a limited number of inquiries and only when timing, scope, and notice line up.

Share the event date, pickup window, and serving size up front—it helps us say yes or no quickly.

What we can do

  • Carrot cake by custom inquiry (see the menu for pricing)
  • Occasional other custom work when the request is simple and clearly scoped
  • Pickup in Liberty with enough lead time for us to plan the bake

What we don’t do

  • Highly detailed or sculpted cakes
  • Last-minute or very complex requests
  • Open-ended requests without clear details

What to include

Event date, pickup day, serving size, flavor, and style notes.
