Custom Orders

Custom cakes when the request fits.

We take on a limited number of custom orders—simple celebration cakes, smaller batches, and things we can do well with enough notice. Here’s what works and what to send us.

What we can do

  • Simple celebration cakes
  • Limited flavor and style options
  • Orders with enough lead time for pickup in Liberty

What we don’t do

  • Highly detailed or sculpted cakes
  • Last-minute or very complex requests
  • Open-ended “can you do anything?” requests—we need a clear idea of what you want

What to include

Include your event date, when you need to pick up, size or servings, flavor, and any style ideas. The more we know, the faster we can tell you if we can make it work.

Send your request

Fill in what you can and submit the form below. Your request is sent to us through the site right away. We’ll get back to you to say whether we can take it on.
