What we build

Deck & Fence Services

From a simple pressure-treated deck to a multi-level composite outdoor living space — we handle the full project.

Composite Decks

Trex, TimberTech, and Fiberon composite decking installed with hidden fasteners. Low maintenance, 25-year fade warranty.

Wood Decks

Pressure-treated pine, cedar, and redwood decks. Classic look at a lower price point, properly sealed for Colorado weather.

Privacy Fencing

Cedar, vinyl, and composite privacy fences up to 8 feet. HOA-compliant styles available throughout the Front Range.

Pergolas & Shade Structures

Free-standing and attached pergolas, louvered roofs, and shade sails. Designed for Colorado sun and wind loads.

Deck Railings

Cable, aluminum, glass, and wood railing systems. Up to code and designed to match your deck's style.

Deck Repair & Restoration

Board replacement, post repair, staining, and structural reinforcement. We can often save a deck that looks like it needs replacing.
