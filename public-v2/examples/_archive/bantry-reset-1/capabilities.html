Capabilities & Equipment

From Concept to Reality

Bantry's current capability set combines machining, fabrication, and finishing processes so customers receive finished parts that are ready to use.

CNC Machining

Most CNC systems were designed for wood and metal, but Bantry applies them to acrylic, lexan/polycarbonate, UHMW, and other plastic materials.

Laser Cutting & Engraving

Bantry highlights a large 43" x 63" bed and dual headed 80-watt laser to improve throughput and reduce waste.

CAD/CAM Design Support

Customers can provide drawings, or Bantry can produce CAD/CAM support files for project development.

Custom Fabrication

Work includes non-standard and difficult items in addition to more common off-the-shelf style components.

Assembly

In-house assembly includes gluing, kitting, and bending support to reduce lead time and simplify downstream handling.

Vacuum Forming

Thermoforming with vacuum and/or pressure forming methods for components built from heated extruded plastic sheets.

Bending & Bonding

Line bending supports different angles and shapes while reducing seams and minimizing glue-intensive joins.

Materials Coverage

Publicly listed materials include acrylic, polycarbonate, PVC, polyethylene, acetals, Noryl, Mylar, and many engineering plastics.
