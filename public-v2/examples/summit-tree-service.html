What we do

Tree Services

From hazardous emergency removal to routine pruning — we handle it safely, completely, and with full cleanup.

Tree Removal

Full removal including haul-off of wood and debris. We section down large trees in tight spaces without damaging structures or landscaping.

Emergency Removal

Storm-downed trees on roofs, fences, power lines, or blocking roads. We respond 24/7 and work with your insurance adjuster.

Available 24 hours · (704) 555-0163

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Crown reduction, deadwood removal, clearance pruning from structures and utility lines. Proper cuts that promote health, not just appearance.

Stump Grinding

Ground to 8–12" below grade with mulch backfill. Stands alone or bundled with removal. We can grind stumps from other companies' work too.

Hazard Assessment

ISA-certified arborist report on tree health and risk. Useful before hurricanes, for property sales, or when you're unsure if a tree needs to come down.

Firewood & Chipping

Split wood left on-site or hauled. Brush and limbs chipped into mulch. We can leave clean chip piles for use in garden beds.
