Featured Project

From Cracked Asphalt to Full Concrete

Completed 2024

3,200 sq ft Driveway — Chicopee, MA

The Problem

Asphalt driveway was cracking, heaving from frost, and had active drainage issues pulling water toward the garage foundation. The homeowner had been patching it for years.

What We Did

Removed all asphalt. Re-graded the sub-base for drainage. Poured 4" reinforced concrete with proper control joints and a broom finish. Installed a new channel drain at the low point near the garage.

Result

Clean, flat surface. Drainage pulls away from the foundation. The homeowner said it was the first winter in eight years they didn't have ice pooling at the garage door.

FAQ

Common Concrete Questions

How long before I can drive on a new concrete driveway?

7 days minimum for foot traffic, 28 days before vehicle traffic. We put this in writing and leave signage so your household knows the timeline.

Do you need to pull a permit for my driveway?

Depends on city and scope. We know the permit requirements for every town we serve. We handle permit applications and inspections when required — included in your quote.

What's the difference between stamped and regular concrete?

Same concrete base — stamped adds a decorative pattern pressed into the surface while it's still wet, plus integral color and a sealer. Costs more but looks significantly better.

Can you replace just part of a cracked driveway?

Sometimes, yes. If the rest of the driveway is structurally sound, we can cut out and replace sections. We'll be honest if a full replacement makes more financial sense long-term.
