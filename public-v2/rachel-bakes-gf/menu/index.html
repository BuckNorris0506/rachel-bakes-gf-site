Menu

Organized for how people actually order.

The menu is grouped by the items customers most often come back for. Availability may change around weekly bakes, holidays, and event weekends.

Pretzel Bites

The most repeatable preorder item. Best for game days, group pickups, family weekends, and occasional extras.

  • Original pretzel bites
  • Seasonal dip add-ons when available
  • Party-size preorder options around high-demand weekends

Cinnamon Rolls

Available around scheduled bake windows, holidays, and limited weekend pickups.

  • Classic gluten-free cinnamon rolls
  • Occasional holiday or seasonal variations
  • Preorder-first when quantities are limited

Cakes

Simple celebration cakes and selected custom requests. This is not an open-everything cake menu.

  • Simple layer cakes and occasion cakes
  • Limited design complexity
  • Notice required before pickup is confirmed

Seasonal Specials

Small-batch items tied to Chiefs game days, holiday weekends, and other seasonal moments.

  • Holiday boxes and event preorders
  • Limited extras announced when available
  • Menu may rotate depending on the week

Holiday / Event Preorders

Holiday and event menus are announced separately when they open. If you are ordering around Thanksgiving, Christmas, game days, birthdays, or school events, check the preorder page for the current timing and request structure.

Custom Orders

Rachel takes selective custom requests when the timing and scope are a fit. Start there only if your request falls within the simple cake / limited custom range.
