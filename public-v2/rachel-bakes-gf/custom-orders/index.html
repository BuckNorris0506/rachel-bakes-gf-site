Custom Orders

Selective custom work, with clearer boundaries.

This page is here to help people understand what Rachel does take on, how much notice is needed, and what details should come with the request.

Good fit custom requests

  • Simple celebration cakes
  • Limited flavor and style variations
  • Pickup-based orders with enough notice

Not a fit

  • Highly detailed sculpted cakes
  • Last-minute complex requests
  • Open-ended “can you do anything?” inquiries

Before you ask

Please include the event date, pickup date, size or servings, flavor, and any style notes so Rachel can tell quickly whether the request is workable.
