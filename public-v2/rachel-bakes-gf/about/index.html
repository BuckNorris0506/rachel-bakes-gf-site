Rachel Bakes GF is a home-based bakery serving local customers in Liberty, Missouri through preorders and pickup. The bakery is strongest when customers can quickly see what is available, understand how pickup works, and send a clear request for the items they actually want.

The focus is on gluten-free favorites people repeat: pretzel bites, cinnamon rolls, seasonal specials, and selective custom work when the request is a fit.

This prototype is designed to make the business feel organized, trustworthy, and easier to order from without turning it into a complicated ecommerce system.
