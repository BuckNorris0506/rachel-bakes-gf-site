Order / Preorder

Clear preorder flow, fewer pickup questions.

Rachel Bakes GF is preorder-first. That means most items are reserved ahead of pickup instead of kept in constant ready-to-go inventory.

How preorder works

  1. 1. Check the current menu and preorder page first.
  2. 2. Submit what you want, your preferred pickup date, and any key notes.
  3. 3. Rachel confirms availability, pickup coordination, and any follow-up details.

Pickup basics

  • Pickup is in Liberty, Missouri.
  • Typical windows are coordinated around bake days, weekends, holidays, and events.
  • Availability may change week to week depending on the menu and order volume.

Best fit orders

Pretzel bites, cinnamon rolls, seasonal releases, and simple celebration items are the easiest orders to place through the regular preorder flow.

Preorder request

This prototype keeps the form simple on purpose. It helps customers send the details Rachel actually needs instead of starting with an open-ended message.

Availability is not guaranteed until the pickup slot is confirmed. Holiday menus, event weekends, and seasonal drops can change the schedule.
