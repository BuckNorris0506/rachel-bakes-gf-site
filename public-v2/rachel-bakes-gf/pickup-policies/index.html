Pickup & Policies

The page customers should check before asking pickup questions.

Practical guidance for pickup, timing, payments, cancellations, and what to expect from a local gluten-free cottage bakery.

Pickup location

Pickup is coordinated in Liberty, Missouri. Exact details are confirmed after the order is accepted so customers are working from the current pickup plan.

Pickup windows

Typical windows are coordinated around the bake schedule, weekends, and holiday/event demand. Pickup is not set as a constant all-week retail schedule.

Ordering timelines

Preorders should be placed early when possible. Holiday menus, game-day bakes, and custom orders may require more notice than the standard weekly items.

Payment expectations

Payment timing is confirmed with the order. Some items may require prepayment or a deposit before the pickup slot is held.

Cancellation / refund clarity

Because this is a preorder bakery, late changes can be difficult once baking is scheduled. Orders canceled close to pickup may not be fully refundable, especially for custom work or holiday orders.

Custom order expectations

Custom orders are accepted selectively. Simple, clearly scoped requests with enough notice are the best fit.

Gluten-free note

Rachel Bakes GF focuses on gluten-free baking. If you have additional allergy questions, include them in the request so they can be addressed before pickup is confirmed.

Cottage bakery note

This prototype uses practical cottage-bakery style policy language for clarity only. Final legal/disclaimer wording should be reviewed against Missouri cottage food requirements before launch.
